## Full description

**Limit 4000 · uses 2713**

```
MacroSync puts your food diary, your gym log and the people who keep you honest in
one app.

TRACK WHAT YOU EAT
Search over three million foods from Open Food Facts, or scan a barcode with your
camera.
Log to breakfast, lunch, dinner or snacks, save the things you eat often to
Favourites, and watch your calorie ring and protein, carb and fat bars fill up as the
day goes on. Tell MacroSync your age, height, weight, activity level and goal, and it
works out sensible daily targets for you — then lets you override every one of them
if you disagree.

TRAIN AND LOG IT
Build routines from a library of 85 exercises, each with step-by-step instructions and
a demonstration. Start a routine and MacroSync tracks the session live: tick sets off
as you go, and it highlights the one you're on, counts your rest between sets, and
estimates the calories you burned from the work you actually did.

Weights carry over from last time, so you never start a set at zero. Group exercises
into supersets and it walks you through them properly — one set of each, back to back,
then round two.

BRING YOUR HISTORY WITH YOU
Already using another app? Export your data and import it. MacroSync reads Hevy and
Strong CSV files, rebuilds your routines from your most recent session of each, and
fills in the weights you were lifting. It matches exercise names to its own library
even when they're written differently, shows you every match before it commits, and
creates anything it doesn't recognise.

SEE THE WHOLE PICTURE
Connect Google Health and your steps, active calories, distance and sleep — including
how long you spent in each sleep stage — appear alongside everything else. It syncs
itself every half hour. Charts show the last 30 days, and a date picker moves the
whole page back through your history. Your health data is visible only to you.

CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS
Set a goal, pick how many times a week everyone has to check in, invite whoever you
like, and hold each other to it. Check in on the honour system or with a photo. There
is a leaderboard, a feed where everyone can comment on each other's check-ins, and
anyone who misses the weekly target is out. No money, no buy-ins — just the group
chat you already have, with a scoreboard attached.

SHARE WHAT WORKS
Post progress, recipes, tips and questions to the community feed. Follow people whose
training you like. Publish a routine you're proud of, or browse what other people have
published and copy it straight into your own list.

MADE TO LIVE IN
Light and dark themes. Choose which notifications you want and whether they arrive by
email or as a push. Everything works offline enough not to fall over on a bad
connection in a gym basement.

MacroSync is free.
```
